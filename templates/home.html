What Hyntor does

Four things, and it tries to do them well.

  • A library that outlives the term

    Notes, slides and handouts stay with the course, not the semester. Join in the spring and you still get everything shared last fall.

  • A tutor that hints first

    Ask about an assignment and you get a nudge, then a bigger one, then a worked explanation - in that order. It is built to help you learn, not to hand in your homework.

  • Answers you can check

    Every answer points at the material it came from, so you can open the page and read it yourself. When a question goes beyond the course files, Hyntor says so.

  • Your university only

    Accounts are tied to a registered university email domain, and a course's materials are visible only to people enrolled in that course.

How it works

Three steps from sign-up to a useful answer.

  1. Sign up and join a course

    Use your university email address, then join the courses you are enrolled in this term.

  2. Share what you have

    Upload PDFs, Word documents and slide decks. Hyntor reads the text and indexes it.

  3. Ask the tutor

    Pick concept mode to understand an idea, or assignment mode for graded work.
